AlligatorHide®

Sunscreen

AlligatorHide™ Sunscreen Topcoat is a high-performance blend of siliconized acrylic components, micro-ceramic spheres, and rubberized elastomeric creating a dynamic roof and wall membrane sealer. An exceptionally durable, UV resistant, fiber reinforced coating with superior adhesion properties. The micro-ceramic spheres will significantly lower the interior temperature of structures by reflecting the sun’s rays and reducing heat by 80%. AlligatorHide™ Sunscreen Topcoat provides a seamless, waterproof, and weather protective sealthat tightly adheres to the substrate.

SURFACE PREPARATION

Surface must be clean, sound, and free of dust, contaminates or loose material. Remove all debris and loose coatings by means of high-pressure water blasting or wire brushing. Any existing mildew on the surface must be completely killed and removed prior to the application of this product. Surface should not accumulate water for more than 48 hours. Coat entire top surface with AlligatorHide Primer. This surface primer is specially designed to penetrate concrete, seal surface porosity, and waterproof the substrate.

USES

Serves as a waterproof coating in exposed situations where tile or flooring is used overtop, or as part of a repair system with other approved compatible coatings.

MIXING - Premix for 3 minutes prior to application only if necessary. Use a drill and Jiffy-type mixer to mix at slow speed (less than 500 rpm) to avoid air entrainment.

PLACEMENT Apply with a 3/4” nap rough surface roller or airless spray. Drying time will be approximately 60 minutes to touch. Cure time is controlled by thickness of the applied coating, relative humidity, and temperature. Wait 2 - 4 hours between coats at 75°F (24°C) degrees or higher at 18 mils or below. Apply only when temperature is above 50°F (10°C). Do not apply if temperature is expected to drop below 50°F (10°C) before the coating has completely dried. Two full coats are needed for proper sealing. Always apply coats in different directions. Never thin or dilute from full strength.

RECOMMENDED EQUIPMENT: Use an airless pump with a pressure of 1,500 to 2,000 psi. A fluid delivery of 1 to 4 gallons per minute with a spray tip of 0.035 to 0.060. The hoses should be at least 3/8” inside diameter and of the high-pressure rate type. If the hose length is more than fifty feet, a larger inside diameter will be required.

JOINTS & REPAIRS - Clean roller and equipment after use with warm soapy water. PACKAGING: 5-gal Pail, 55-gal Drum, and 250-gal Tote.

CLEAN-UP - As secondary protection under tiles in wet areas e.g. bathrooms, food processing areas, balconies, etc.

PACKAGING - 5-gal Pail, 55-gal Drum, and 250-gal Tote.
