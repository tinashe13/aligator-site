ABOUT US

Our AlligatorHide brand is built on over 20 years of expertise in delivering high-quality roofing solutions. Our products were developed and rigorously tested in the unforgiving conditions of West Texas, where strong winds, hail, extreme heat, and freezing temperatures push materials to their limits. The goal behind AlligatorHide is simple: to create roofing products that can withstand the harshest environments while remaining cost-effective and sustainable. When you choose AlligatorHide, you’re choosing a brand dedicated to innovation, durability, and lasting peace of mind.

Primer

Eco Barrier Primer

AlligatorHide® Eco Barrier Primer is a flexible, eco-friendly waterproofing membrane for residential and commercial use. Easy to apply, it provides durable protection on roofs, walls, floors, and basements, compatible with various surfaces like concrete and metal. Ideal for repairs or new projects, it requires a UV-protective topcoat for exterior use.

Topcoat

AlligatorHide™ Sunscreen

AlligatorHide™ Sunscreen Topcoat is a high-performance blend of siliconized acrylic components, micro-ceramic spheres, and rubberized elastomeric creating a dynamic roof and wall membrane sealer. An exceptionally durable, UV resistant, fiber reinforced coating with superior adhesion properties. The micro-ceramic spheres will significantly lower the interior temperature of structures by reflecting the sun’s rays and reducing heat by 80%.
